# Tiwa 'N' Tiwa — one-page website audit

*Prepared for the owners of Tiwa 'N' Tiwa, 34 Peckham High Street.*

## What people find today

| | Today | Proposed |
|---|---|---|
| Web address | A `menu-world.com` subdomain you don't own | Your own domain, e.g. `tiwantiwa.co.uk` |
| First impression | Generic template, no photos of your food | Big photography of your suya, jollof and fish |
| Menu | Not published anywhere official | Full menu with descriptions a first-timer understands |
| Reviews | 4.3★ from 235+ Google reviews, invisible on the site | Shown on the homepage with real quotes |
| Press | The Infatuation reviewed you in 2024, nowhere on the site | Quoted on the homepage |
| Booking | Works, but buried | "Book a table" on every screen |
| Delivery | Two duplicate Uber Eats stores | One link, plus Deliveroo / Just Eat if you use them |
| Phone | Works | Tap to call and WhatsApp on every page |
| Google search | No site for "Nigerian restaurant Peckham" | Built-in Restaurant schema and local SEO |
| Mobile | Template, slow behind a security check | Loads in under a second, designed for phones first |

## What the new site is

- **Home**: hero, six signature dishes, "how to eat here" guide for new customers, reviews, your story, group bookings, hours and map.
- **Menu**: every section, prices, hot / vegetarian tags, allergen note.
- Hosting that costs nothing, a domain that costs about £10 a year, and a site you keep.
